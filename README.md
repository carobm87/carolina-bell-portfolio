## AI-powered product I designed and built

### OrbitFlow Intelligence Hub

I designed and built a React application that turns a plain-language client intake into a structured, scored engagement decision by calling a large language model against an opinionated scoring rubric I designed. The product converts an ambiguous request into a practical decision about fit, scope, risks, next questions, and a recommended starting point. The interesting work is not the API call. It is the rubric design, including the deliberate choice to make the model favor small, concrete, well-owned problems over vague ambitious ones. It is also the guardrails, including gated pipeline transitions and encoded scope boundaries, that keep the output usable instead of merely plausible. The result is a repeatable qualification system for deciding where an advisory engagement should start and whether it should proceed at all.

The source is private, and I am glad to walk through the architecture and design thinking live.

## Product thinking, worked examples

For a senior PM interview panel, I built and presented two open-ended business cases, one on fixed-price margin loss and one on escalation and crisis management. These are hypothetical cases, not client results, and they are included to demonstrate how I diagnose under real constraints.

My five-step method:

1. What is actually happening
2. The real problem or pattern underneath
3. The priority
4. The actions
5. The outcome

The through-line is consistent: separate the delivery problem from the business decision, make the invisible visible with data, and protect both the business and the team.

Anonymized examples are available on request.
